About This Exhibit

This exhibition explores how video games represent mineralogy, comparing virtual minerals with real specimens from the Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County collection.

In Stardew Valley, players collect minerals through mining and geode cracking, donating them to Gunther's museum in Pelican Town. The game includes 6 different minerals with varying rarity and uses.

Curated by Dr. Aaron Celestian
Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County
